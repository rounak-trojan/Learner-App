Attendance Dump

Upload a login/logout correction CSV (city_name, centre_name, centre_uid, learner_uid, roll_number, Day of dates, log_in_time, log_out_time).

This overrides, not merges: for every (learner, date) in the file, any attendance already on record for that learner on that day — whether captured by the face scanner or a previous dump — is replaced with exactly what this file says for that day. A row with only a log-in or only a log-out still fully replaces that day's record for that learner. Rows for a learner_uid not enrolled in this system are skipped.
